# Stage 8 — Intermediate Zoom Out Report

Target: full file. Reviewer: `egm-zoom-out`, with the Stage 5 and Stage 6 reports as context.

1. **BLOCKER — The log is not the only writer, so the rebuild destroys data it cannot see.** `record_claim` is emitted only by label and the one-time backfill (line 148); the STT importer inserts Records, Activities, and Tags directly (`stt_import_service.dart:458, 200, 229`); first-run seeding creates five Activities with no event (`main.dart:180`). A rebuild either deletes imported Records or leaves them unclaimed, breaking coverage. Fix: make the log the single write path before Stage 1, or add an explicit "adopt unclaimed Record" step.
2. **BLOCKER — Day has no identity.** The unit rebuilt is "each Day touched" (line 161) and coverage is "Records within each Day" (line 207), but no `day_key` exists; `Day` is defined by the computing device's zone (`CONTEXT.md:9–10`). A phone that crossed zones makes different Days than the Mac, so CUJ 2 fails the convergence goal. Fix: put `day_key` (first local midnight instant plus zone) on claims and Records; group, rebuild, and check by it. This dissolves the random-half-id, unclaimed-span, and suppressed-edit findings together.
3. **MAJOR — A watch label is resolved by a different policy than the same fact from the Mac.** Peer claims clip and suppress; watch actions are rejected when earlier than the phone's Lap start (line 201), which is the normal case after hours offline. Fix: route watch actions through the same resolver; reserve rejection for undecodable payloads and unknown Activities.
4. **MAJOR — Full replay in one request livelocks.** A restored device clears cursors and sends its full log (line 193) in one `POST /sync` that must be size-bounded (line 559) while the log reaches tens of MB (line 343); the cursor advances only on a completed apply, so the retry never sends less. Fix: cursor-paginated batches.
5. **MAJOR — No execution model for sync or backup on Android.** Triggers and the five-minute snapshot assume a running isolate (lines 194, 209); no foreground service, `WorkManager`, or boot receiver is planned, and no accepted risk covers it. Fix: scope to foreground-only and say so, or add a service and its cost.
6. **MAJOR — The recoverable copy is as fresh as the last promotion.** The five-minute tier is local and dies with the uninstall (line 35 vs 209); promotion is hourly and on pause, and uninstall is neither. Fix: state the real window and promote before destructive actions.
7. **MAJOR — The whole conflict apparatus exists for one journey that contradicts the usage pattern.** Five of six CUJs are single-writer; CUJ 4 requires a partition between two devices described as sitting together (line 15). Fix: instrument real overlaps before building the merge, or cut to the queue model.
8. **MAJOR — Stage order pays the log's cost before anything reads it.** Stage 1 ships v10, backfill, and emission with no consumer until Stage 4, while backup and restore (Stage 2) depend on none of it. Fix: ship watch fixes first, then backup, then v10 with its consumer in one release.
